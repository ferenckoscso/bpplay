The real music selector, in your browser.

This is the actual zenevalaszto.py browsing experience, reimplemented for the web: the same keys, the same screens, a short real (licensed) preview clip. Click into the terminal and use your keyboard, or the on-screen keys on mobile.

Note: no browser audio path (Web Audio, HTML5 <audio>) is bit-perfect; every browser resamples through its own OS audio stack. This demo faithfully shows the navigation experience; the audio quality itself is not the same as bpplay's native, bit-perfect signal path. Native DSD playback is not possible in a browser at all; DSD-format demo tracks are shown here as their PCM-decoded equivalent. The output (DAC) list and the USB/NFS/SMB mounting shown here are simulated with mock data, exactly as the real screens behave; no actual hardware or network is contacted from your browser.
