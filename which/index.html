Raspberry Pi 3

The Pi 3 isn't supported

The bpplay Pi appliance edition only runs on the Raspberry Pi 4 or 5; the Pi 3 is not supported and will not work.

If you have a spare Windows/Linux PC lying around instead, the x64 editions might be a good alternative.

x64 kiosk edition → x64 desktop edition →
Our recommendation

Get a Raspberry Pi 4 or 5

It is the simplest, cheapest, purpose-built way to try bpplay: small, quiet, and headless by design. If you would rather repurpose a spare PC instead, or want more storage or performance, the x64 kiosk edition is a strong alternative.
